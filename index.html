How do I book a cricket slot?

Select your date and preferred hourly slot in the booking section above, then click "Continue on WhatsApp". Our team confirms availability and reserves your time.

What are the cricket slot rates?

Daytime slots (6 AM – 6 PM) are ₹800/hour. Night slots under floodlights are ₹1,000/hour on weekdays and ₹1,200/hour on weekends.

Can I host a birthday or private squad celebration?

Yes! We offer customised private packages with cake cutting areas, sound system, party lighting, and exclusive turf usage. Submit an Event Booking for a personalised quote.

Do you provide cricket bats and balls?

Yes — genuine English willow bats, stitched match balls, stumps, and protective gear are provided at the venue.
